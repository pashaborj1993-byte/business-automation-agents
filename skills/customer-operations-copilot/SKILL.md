# Customer operations copilot

Improve speed and consistency without turning customer support into an unaccountable chatbot.

## Define service boundaries

Name the channels, service hours, supported topics, customer segments, source-of-truth systems, and escalation owners. Distinguish informational requests from actions that change an order, booking, account, money, contract, or customer access.

## Build the operating loop

1. Ingest the request and attach the permitted customer and order context.
2. Detect language, topic, urgency, sentiment, and whether human review is required.
3. Retrieve only approved knowledge and cite or link the source in the agent workspace.
4. Draft a response or next action with no invented policy, pricing, availability, or refund commitment.
5. Route the item to the right queue with a clear reason and deadline.
6. Record the final human action and use resolved cases to identify knowledge gaps.

## Approval and safety

The system may acknowledge receipt and send pre-approved, low-risk status updates if the business explicitly allows it. A human must approve refunds, credits, cancellations, legal statements, account changes, unusual discounts, sensitive-data requests, and any message the agent marks uncertain.

## Measure

Track first response, time to resolution, reopen rate, escalation rate, customer satisfaction, and repeat-contact rate. Audit a sample of automated or drafted responses weekly. If quality or escalation rate deteriorates, narrow the automation scope before adding more channels.
