# Lead-to-quote automation

Make every genuine enquiry visible, qualified, and owned until it is won, lost, or disqualified.

## Establish the commercial workflow

Map every entry point: website form, email, WhatsApp, referral, marketplace, phone call, or social message. Define the source of truth for the lead record and the exact fields required to decide whether the business should pursue it.

Use explicit stages: received, acknowledged, qualified, discovery booked, proposal drafted, proposal approved, sent, won, lost, or dormant. Do not treat an AI confidence score as a qualification decision without business rules and a human owner.

## Automate safely

Automate capture, duplicate detection, enrichment from permitted sources, routing, scheduling links, reminders, and draft replies. Draft a proposed response using the prospect's request, relevant services, and transparent assumptions.

Require approval before sending a proposal, committing price, promising scope or dates, adding a prospect to a marketing list, or transmitting sensitive customer material. Preserve an audit trail of who approved the final quote.

## Handle exceptions

Escalate incomplete requests, spam, conflicting contact details, unusually large opportunities, regulated work, and requests outside the service boundary. Never silently discard a lead that appears genuine; assign it to a human review queue.

## Measure and improve

Track first-response time, qualification rate, discovery-booked rate, proposal turnaround, win rate, and lead leakage. Compare against a baseline after 30 days. Recommend the next improvement from observed drop-off rather than adding features by default.
